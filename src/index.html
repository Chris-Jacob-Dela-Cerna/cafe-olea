THE COOKIE EVERYONE QUEUES FOR.

When our Dubai chewy cookies come out of the oven, the line usually follows. Thick, soft and properly chewy, filled with pistachio cream and crisp toasted kataifi, it's the one thing our regulars never leave without. Baked fresh each morning, and when they're gone, they're gone.

REAL INGREDIENTS, REAL CRUNCH.

Our pistachio cream is made in-house from roasted pistachios, white chocolate and a touch of tahini. The kataifi is toasted slowly in butter until golden, then folded in right before baking so every bite stays crisp. We use dark chocolate by the block, never chips, and finish each cookie with flaky sea salt. And once you've tried the original, ask about Dubai Bianco, Dubai Caramello and Dubai Nero.

No shortcuts. Just the Olea way.

WONDERING WHAT MAKES OUR COOKIES SO THICK AND CHEWY?

It starts with three simple things: brown butter, quality chocolate and time. Our dough is mixed by hand and rested for 48 hours, which deepens the flavour, brings out the caramel notes and gives the cookie its signature chew. Our fillings and flavours change with the seasons, but every recipe follows the same rule: make it slowly, make it properly, make it worth the wait.

A STORY IN EVERY CUP.

INSPIRED BY OLD CAFÉS. MADE FOR TODAY.

Picture a quiet corner café in a European city, with marble tables, bentwood chairs, the hiss of the espresso machine and a glass case of pastries by the door. That feeling, unhurried and a little bit romantic, is where Olea began.

In 2021, two friends who had spent years collecting café memories from Rome to Vienna to Paris decided to bring that culture home. Olea takes its name from the Latin word for the olive tree, a symbol of peace, patience and things that grow slowly.

Today, with our flagship on Maginhawa Street and a second home in Tagaytay, we're proud to share that slower way of doing things with more people. More than a place to get a coffee, Olea is built on craft, comfort and a love of the everyday ritual. Pull up a chair and stay awhile, because every cup tells a story.
